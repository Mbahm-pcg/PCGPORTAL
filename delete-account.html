PCG Unified Operations Portal — People Capital Group

The PCG Unified Operations Portal (including the "PCG Pulse" Android/iOS app) is an internal operations tool for People Capital Group employees. Accounts are provisioned and managed by our IT/HR team — there is no self-service sign-up, so account deletion is handled the same way.

How to request deletion

  1. Email ahmed@peoplecapitalgroup.com from the address associated with your account (or have your manager submit the request on your behalf).
  2. Include your full name and the store or district you're associated with, so we can locate the correct account.
  3. We will confirm once your account has been removed, typically within 5 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record — name, email address, phone number, and login credentials — is permanently removed from our systems.

What may be retained

  • Operational records you contributed to while employed — such as chat messages, ticket comments, task completions, and audit log entries — are retained for business record-keeping and are no longer associated with your name or account after deletion.
