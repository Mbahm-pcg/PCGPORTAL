Privacy Policy

PCG Unified Operations Portal — People Capital Group · Last updated August 2026

The PCG Unified Operations Portal ("the Portal", including the "PCG Pulse" Android/iOS app) is an internal operations tool built for People Capital Group employees — store managers, district managers, maintenance and construction staff, and corporate office staff. It is not available to the public and does not collect data from customers or general users; every account is provisioned by our IT/HR team.

Third parties we work with

We use the following service providers to run the Portal. They process data only to provide their service to us — we do not sell data or share it for advertising.

  • Paycor — payroll/HR system of record (we pull data from Paycor; we don't send it there).
  • Twilio — delivers SMS alerts to your phone number, if you opt in.
  • Resend / Google Workspace SMTP / Gmail API — sends notification emails and syncs a shared company inbox displayed in the Portal's Email workspace.
  • Google Sign-In — optional login method; shares your name/email with Google as the identity provider.
  • Netlify — hosts the Portal and its serverless functions.
  • Neon (Postgres) — stores account, ticket, chat, and audit data.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — powers the "Orion" AI assistant feature; only the specific question/report data you ask about is sent, never your full account record.

Security

All traffic to and from the Portal is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), including the database connection. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Accounts lock after repeated failed login attempts.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data for as long as you're an active employee/user of the Portal. You (or your admin, on your behalf) can request that your account and its associated personal data be deleted at any time — see our Delete Account & Data page for how. Some operational records you contributed (chat messages, ticket comments, audit entries) are retained for business record-keeping after your account is deleted, no longer linked to your name.

Children's privacy

The Portal is an internal workplace tool for employees and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll post it here and notify you in the Portal.
