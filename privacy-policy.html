Privacy Policy

PCG Unified Operations Portal — People Capital Group LLC · Last updated November 13, 2026

The PCG Unified Operations Portal ("the Portal", including the "PCG Pulse" Android/iOS app) is an internal tool People Capital Group LLC ("PCG", "we") uses to run its Dunkin' restaurants. It is used by PCG staff (store managers, district managers, directors, office, maintenance and construction staff) whose accounts are set up by our IT/HR team. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from restaurant guests.

Some PCG employees who don't have a Portal account also use one part of it: a private link, sent to them by email, to read and sign a work document (see "Electronic signatures" below). This policy covers them too.

Electronic signatures

Some forms are signed on screen in the Portal, or from a private link we email to the person who needs to sign. Before signing from a link you confirm the last 4 digits of your cell number and agree to sign electronically. You don't have to: you can ask your supervisor or HR to sign on paper instead, and you can ask HR for a free paper copy of any document you signed. The signed document records when and how it was signed. Links are private to you, expire after 3 days and stop working after 5 wrong tries.

Emails and texts

We send work notices by email and text: for example a document to sign, an approval request, a schedule or labor alert, or a reminder. Texts are short work notices; we never send marketing. Signing-link emails record whether they were delivered, bounced or opened, so we can follow up if one doesn't reach you. To stop texts, tell your supervisor or HR and they will reach you another way.

Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run the Portal for us, only for that purpose:

  • Paycor, our payroll/HR system. We read employee records from Paycor, and we send approved hours and tips into Paycor's payroll grid, where payroll staff review them before payroll is run.
  • Google Workspace: Google Drive stores signed HR forms in company folders; Gmail and Google's email service send some notices; Google Sign-In is an optional way to sign in.
  • Resend sends emails and tells us whether they were delivered or opened.
  • Textbelt and Twilio send text messages.
  • Netlify hosts the Portal; Neon stores its database.
  • Paradox, our hiring system, sends us applicant and interview details.
  • Anthropic (Claude) powers the "Orion" assistant. Only the data needed to answer a question or build a report is sent.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or with our franchisor or advisers where needed to run the business.

Who can see what

Access follows your role. A store manager sees their own store, a district manager or director their own districts, and only executives and IT/HR see every store. HR forms are seen by the person who made them, the people who approve them, and HR.

Security

All traffic to and from the Portal is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), including the database connection. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins, and signing links are single-use and expire.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while you use the Portal. Signed HR documents and payroll-related records are kept as long as the law requires us to keep employment records, and as long as we need them for the business. You or your admin can ask for your account to be deleted; see Delete Account & Data. Some records you took part in (signed documents, chat messages, ticket comments, audit entries) are kept after an account is deleted, because they are business or employment records.

Employees under 18

The Portal is a workplace tool and is not meant for children. Some of our employees are 16 or 17, and the Portal handles their work records (including date of birth) so we can follow the labor rules for minors.

Your choices

You can ask HR to see or correct information we hold about you, for a paper copy of a document you signed, or to stop receiving texts. Location sharing is always your choice.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let Portal users know in the Portal.
