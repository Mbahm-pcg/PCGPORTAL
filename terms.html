1. Your account

  • Your account is for you alone. Don't share your password or let anyone else use your account, and don't use anyone else's.
  • Tell IT right away if you think someone else has used your account or knows your password.
  • Shared store tablets and kiosk screens are for the purpose they were set up for only.
  • Your access ends when your job ends or your role changes, and PCG may limit or remove it at any time.

2. Use it for work, within your role

  • Use the Portal only for PCG business.

3. Confidential information

Employee records, pay, hours, HR forms, sales and other business information in the Portal are confidential. Keep them confidential while you work at PCG and after you leave. This doesn't limit any right you have under the law, including discussing your own pay and working conditions.

4. Forms, signatures and accuracy

  • When you sign a form in the Portal (on screen, with your finger or mouse), that is your signature, with the same effect as signing on paper.
  • By signing or submitting a form you confirm that what it says is true and complete to the best of your knowledge. This matters most for anything that affects pay, such as missing hours, time off, pay increases and tips.
  • Have the employee sign on your screen only when they are with you and have read the document. If they aren't there, use the emailed signing link. Never sign for someone else.
  • An employee may choose to sign on paper instead of electronically. Respect that, and give them a paper copy when they ask.

6. Reports and the Orion assistant

Answers from the Orion AI assistant can be wrong; check them before acting on them.

7. Activity is recorded

The Portal records sign-ins and actions (who did what, and when) for security and accountability. Don't expect privacy in what you do in the Portal. Location is only shared when you choose to share it.
